# My Personal AI Usage Rules

## Rule 1 - What I will never paste

Passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys, `.env` contents, customer data, or other confidential information.

## Rule 2 - What I will always verify before accepting

AI-generated code, commands, security claims, test results, and factual statements that affect decisions or production systems.

## Rule 3 - How I will record AI contributions

I will document material AI assistance in commits, pull requests, or project notes, including what AI proposed and what I independently reviewed or changed.
